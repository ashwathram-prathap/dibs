# Dibs marks

The logo is the lowercase `dibs.` wordmark with its vermilion square. Use it in the shared header, footer, certificate corner and social images. Never typeset uppercase DIBS as a substitute logo or page headline.

The uppercase DIBS stamp records the game action: a claim, a golden claim or a certificate. Its border, ink and slight impression belong to an actual filing. It is not a decorative headline.

The founder has a gold name and the explicit FOUNDER label. This is a server-assigned identity role, not a golden ticket. It has no star, no golden-ticket stamp and no gameplay privileges.
