Dibs answer-register sources and reuse

GeoNames cities1000: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Includes all rows in the downloaded cities1000 extract, with alternate and ASCII names. Same normalized names are merged, preferring the most populous place. GeoNames https://www.geonames.org/.

Princeton WordNet 3.0. Copyright 2006 Princeton University. All rights reserved.
Full required license and disclaimer: /data/wordnet-license.txt
English common animal, plant and food words; taxonomic Latin names are not the primary answers. https://wordnet.princeton.edu/

IOC World Bird List, Gill, F., Donsker, D. & Rasmussen, P. (Eds).
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
https://www.worldbirdnames.org/ . Source version is recorded in each catalog build's review notes.

Open Food Facts contributors. Contains information from Open Food Facts, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, with individual contents under the Database Contents License.
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
The derived food database is available as machine-readable rows and alias-key shards under /catalog/foods/ (see manifest.json for the immutable version directory). The derived database is offered under ODbL 1.0. These files contain no player information or golden status.

ISO 3166 data: iso-3166, copyright Titus Wormer, MIT. /data/iso-license.txt
https://github.com/wooorm/iso-3166

Wikipedia PageViewInfo measurements: https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php
Article titles and measured pageviews only; common names remain the answer, even when a popularity measurement resolves a redirect. Unavailable readership is recorded as unknown, not a fabricated observation. Editorial common-name supplements by Dibs.

GBIF Secretariat (2023). GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. Checklist dataset https://doi.org/10.15468/39omei accessed 2026-11-22. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Full 2023 backbone Darwin Core archive, https://hosted-datasets.gbif.org/datasets/backbone/current/backbone.zip . All English VernacularName records were joined to Taxon kingdom/class. Changes: common-name selection, normalization, synonym deduplication and unsuitable-term filtering. The legacy backbone is frozen at 2023; it is merged with current IOC 15.2 and USDA PLANTS rather than represented as a current taxonomic authority. Scientific names are lookup aliases. GBIF's current taxonomic authority is Catalogue of Life XR.

USDA, NRCS. 2026. The PLANTS Database (https://plants.usda.gov, accessed 22 November 2026). National Plant Data Team, Greensboro, NC USA.
Complete plant list, https://plants.sc.egov.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/Txt/plantlst.txt . Plant lists and text are not copyrighted and are free for any use; no images are included. Conditions: https://plants.usda.gov/assets/docs/PLANTS_Help_Document.pdf (Plant Data Use).

Wikipedia contributors: culinary dish, fruit and vegetable lists; CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Each supplemental row retains its source page and article title; full lists are extracted, normalized and deduplicated. Pageviews use Wikimedia PageViewInfo. Lists and their extraction audit are recorded in review/round8/curated-source-extraction.json.
GeoNames alternateNamesV2: full English alternate-name and English Wikipedia-link extraction, same CC BY 4.0 attribution as cities1000.

Mammal Diversity Database (2026), Version 2.5, 6,904 species, American Society of Mammalogists. CC BY 4.0. https://www.mammaldiversity.org/about/ and https://zenodo.org/records/17033774 . Complete CSV mirrored by the project's official GitHub repository; English main/other common names merged, scientific names retained as aliases, normalized and deduplicated. Myotis hajastanicus is treated as a synonym of Myotis davidii per the MDD taxonomy notes; its documented common name Armenian whiskered bat is also accepted.

OurAirports: complete airports.csv, public domain, https://ourairports.com/data/ and https://github.com/datasets/airport-codes . Official source mirror: https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data ; excludes heliports/balloonports and retains current and closed airports, airfields and seaplane bases. Names and identifiers normalized and deduplicated.
GeoNames allCountries: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ under CC BY 4.0. Complete feature-code extracts for mountains/ranges/peaks/volcanoes; rivers/streams/wadis; lakes/reservoirs; islands/groups/islets; and current/former rail/metro stations/stops. All eligible rows considered, English preferred and alternate names merged; one answer per normalized name.
