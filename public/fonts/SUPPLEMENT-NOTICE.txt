The distributed Google Fonts subsets omit U+02BB, U+1E90 and U+1E91.
This separate, internally renamed font reuses Archivo's left single quote for the
ʻokina and combines its Z/z and circumflex outlines for Ẑ/ẑ. No other glyphs change.
Rebuild regular and black OTF files with:
  npx tsx scripts/build-font-supplement.ts

The browser loads these three glyphs only when required. The Worker uses the
black supplement alongside the official Latin, extended Latin and Vietnamese
subsets. scripts/verify-fonts.ts audits all catalog names in original and uppercase.
