Manual review corrected PNG answer rules that touched the tops of letters; final exports leave an ink gap. Mobile content flows directly from the claim form into the handle/filings. Highlighted time text uses ink. Certificate date-to-holder spacing is tighter. Both themes passed axe WCAG AA checks in the device flow. The real local claim recording includes three stamp moments and one taken strike; first stamp lasts 1.3 seconds, later stamps 1.05 seconds, with reduced-motion support.

Lighthouse mobile, simulated Slow 4G: performance 100, accessibility 100, best practices 100. SEO intentionally remains lower because this private preview is noindex. Physical keyboards, native share sheets and social webviews still need the owner's phone checks.
