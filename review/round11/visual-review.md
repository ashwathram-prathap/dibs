## Notes by screen

- **closed:** Large vermilion stamp restored on phone and desktop; explicit drop time and header controls.
- **play:** Huge Archivo category retained; plain stock, crisp primary action, focused one-card flow.
- **mid-round:** Stable card and explicit progress; no premature ledger clutter.
- **day:** Ink answer names, signature, separate counters; desktop companions use the right column.
- **five-strikes:** Misses are legible and RESERVED is plain; right column balances the completed file.
- **gold:** Compact physical stamp retains prominence without an empty full-width stamp box.
- **certificate:** Large answer and original ink rules retained; hand signature adds one restrained mark.
- **archive:** Same oversized wordmark and explicitly labelled specimen; larger companion fills the grid.
- **office:** Stronger Dibs headline; FAQ spacing and sources split into clear sections. Long legal copy remains the weakest mobile section.
- **office-expanded:** Five short rules, baseline-aligned numbers and a padded drawn disclosure chevron.
- **admin-live:** R7 has no Live route: its 404 shell is the visual reference, not an equivalent feature.
- **calendar:** R7 has no calendar route: compare the shared shell only.
- **admin-live-open:** Authenticated practice metrics use aligned rules and large numbers. R7 route absent.
- **calendar-open:** Authenticated calendar fills the container with deliberate form boxes. R7 route absent.
- **stats:** Simple administrative form; header and footer share the same centre line.
- **error:** Deliberately sparse recovery screen, aligned with the shared container.

## Anti-AI checklist

- No gradients, glass, glow, blob backgrounds, or decorative engraving/seal. Gold uses one short glint, not a surface gradient.
- No feature-card hero, pill controls, rounded soft-shadow cards, emoji, or decorative icon library.
- Layout follows a strict shared grid; desktop certificates and Office sections have useful companions.
- Archivo, Instrument Sans and Plex remain the visible typefaces. Drawn rules, stamps and labels supply deliberate detail.
- No filler copy or stock illustrations. Archive specimens and local fixtures are explicitly labelled; no fabricated live players.
- Dark surfaces are carbon copy (#16150F / #1F1E18); CALL DIBS is #E1462B in both themes.

Weakest remaining layouts: the long mobile Office privacy section; the intentionally sparse 404; the dense 60-day calendar. They meet the baseline and accessibility checks.
