# Catalog decisions and source corrections

- The last instruction says not to change the schedule. Existing dates and approvals stay unchanged; passing the new gate is recorded separately. The earlier proposed seven-day replacement is not applied.
- The full ISO set has 249 countries and territories. The title and rule say countries **and territories**; entries are not fabricated to reach 300.
- Complete GBIF, IOC, USDA, WordNet, GeoNames and OFF extractions replace the query-order cap. MDD 2.5 adds all 6,904 mammal taxa and current English names. Complete culinary and garden common-name lists supplement regional food, fruit and plant gaps; the initial independent misses remain in separate audit files.
- GeoNames identities merge by normalized canonical name only. An alternate name shared by two differently named places does not merge those places. All primary/ASCII names, English alternate names and Latin-script source aliases are considered, with canonical names taking precedence in an alias collision. Administrative qualifiers in independent Wikipedia city labels are removed for the explicit name-only game rule.
- Majmaah is accepted for Al Majma'ah; Shire for Inda Silasē / Shire Inda Selassie. The independent Wikidata article labels and the GeoNames records refer to these same places. These are documented alternate-name repairs, not replacement samples.
- Biological reference records use their scientific-name Wikipedia article (including redirects) for readership when available. General nouns retain common-name articles. Domain checks reject unrelated homonyms such as plant Panda → giant panda, plant Koala → marsupial, and animal Cricket → sport. Source-qualified unknown measurements remain unknown.
- Domestic cat/house cat, domestic dog/pooch, chicken/domestic fowl/cockerel/pullet/hen/rooster, tiger/tigress and peacock/peafowl/peahen/peachick are explicit common-name aliases. They do not occupy repeated house slots through separate aliases of one article.
- Pageviews are measured, not invented scores. The verified launch Animals, Plants and Birds retain their October 23–November 20 daily window. Other completed catalogs use the full October 2026 human-readership dump, all access methods and null page IDs included, divided by 31 days. Seasonal or news-driven answers can outrank familiar ones; Axolotl is not manually excluded from the measured house.
- The public static indexes carry accepted names and obscurity only, with no golden flags. The server's commitment and unrevealed selections remain private. Cached lookups and cold index downloads are reported separately.
- Name matching preserves plural letters in proper names, so Cars/Car and Dreams/Dream remain distinct. Biological and food registers explicitly carry singular/plural aliases; canonical nouns reserve their inflections before ambiguous source aliases. Legacy D1 registers retain the old matching fold until replaced.
- Shared vernacular aliases do not merge distinct animal taxa. Explicit scientific identifiers and documented editorial renames can identify the same entity. Invalid replacement-character labels are rejected instead of becoming corrupted answers.
- When a biological subtype redirects to the article belonging to a different accepted common name, that article's readership is attributed to its own answer. The subtype stays accepted with unknown popularity. This prevents repeated house slots from several taxa pointing to one general article.
- New articles with no numeric readership are verified with 30-day, 60-day and REST requests. Confirmed absence is recorded in `pageview-gaps.json`; these answers remain accepted with unknown popularity. Later real data takes precedence.
- The official Cloudflare dummy credentials are confined to an explicit practice-only build, the exact practice origin, and the staging flag. Production still requires the real hostname and claim action. Practice replay resets every five answers to respect the existing five-identities-per-connection rule; production limits are unchanged.
- Wikidata candidates use English article titles plus English and multilingual default labels/aliases. English-only labels are not required, because many well-known names use multilingual defaults. Full classification extraction is count-checked when split into subclass groups.
- Wikipedia property continuations retain descriptions and page properties from earlier responses. Food and airport homonyms require matching domain descriptions. OurAirports free-form keywords are not aliases: they can contain nearby rivers, events or generic aviation tags. Airport codes, source names and verified article redirects supply aliases instead.
- Independent pools resolve article redirects before reading popularity and scope. Existing frozen samples are never redrawn; the first complete Music groups and Paintings draws are preserved separately. Entries pointing to a solo artist or another visual-art medium are excluded before these new draws.
- The runtime uses the full sorted key index. The old 256 per-category key shards are unused and are no longer shipped, reducing Pages file count without removing answers or aliases.
- Song and novel imports include form-of-creative-work (`P7937`) as well as instance (`P31`) classifications. Johnny B. Goode and Material Girl are typed as audio tracks; The Hobbit and Dune are typed as literary works. Their song/novel form is a separate property. The full form-class union is reimported; audit examples do not become manual membership patches. Songs & compositions explicitly includes musical theatre and operas.
- A Windows-1252/UTF-8 decoding repair preserves the launch Animals readership window. A duplicated repaired vernacular merges, and a field-locality description misfiled as an animal name is removed. Animals becomes 89,122; the measured ten house words remain unchanged.
- Source work titles containing emoji-capable marks use readable transcriptions or matching plain-text aliases. Original spellings remain accepted keys. Trademark marks, mathematical symbols and non-BMP symbols are included in the glyph audit.
- Museum membership also includes the complete Wikipedia museum-infobox reference, not selected coverage misses. A full traversal added 1,449 raw records; previous redirects and independently drawn samples are retained.
- Wikidata mountain ranges and summits are not subclasses of mountain. The geographic complement explicitly includes mountains, ranges, summits and volcanoes; unfinished classification revisions are gated off even when the GeoNames primary reference is complete.
- Ordinary source honorifics and naval prefixes/hull qualifiers also produce accepted aliases. Canonical names and the frozen independent samples remain unchanged. The complete P106 normal-rank supplement is count-checked, including a same-snapshot membership/count query when Wikidata changes during extraction.
- Castles now merges the complete GeoNames CSTL/FORT/PAL reference with the full Wikidata castle, fortification, palace and manor classification. Its Wikipedia aliases and the full station-complement aliases are verified. Stations passes 98.77% obvious coverage and all 50 independent samples; Castles passes 98.08% obvious coverage, with five independent misses retained for review.
- GeoNames feature-code review found omitted crater/oxbow/underground lake and islet/artificial/mangrove island codes. A full supplemental extraction preserves the existing enriched primary references. It adds 2,698 lake records, 12,742 watercourse records and 17,003 island records before cleaning; source feature definitions are https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/featureCodes_en.txt. Sections of lakes/islands and stream bends/mouths are excluded.

- Complete castle title-inventory checks reject schools, battles and other subjects merely located at a castle. Source building-type words such as Castelo/Castillo/Château are accepted with ordinary English name order. The original samples remain unchanged.
- Paintings without English articles exposed a genuine completeness gap; diptych/triptych preferred statements also hide valid normal-rank painting membership. A full named, non-deprecated-statement extraction checked all 1,125,346 independently counted candidates across 279 classes and retained 723,650 named source records before cleaning and deduplication. Existing article aliases and original-title evidence are preserved. All 723,650 source records have verified English/multilingual aliases and native-title properties; all 12,227 English article associations were checked. The cleaned catalog has 516,458 titles, 99.05% obvious coverage and 49/50 independent matches. Remaining misses stay visible and the category stays unscheduled.

- Painting article redirects require painting/artwork scope. A mural link redirected to its subject’s biography; 396 unrelated or unverified article associations were excluded from readership and their aliases rebuilt from Wikidata alone. Verified artwork names remain accepted. This prevents a popular subject’s biography from determining house words.
